**Apex Finance & Stocks** is a fast, ad-free Android market tracker and technical charting app for **Global Equities (US NASDAQ/NYSE)**, **Indian Markets (NSE/BSE Nifty & Sensex)**, **Cryptocurrencies**, and **Commodities**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **⭐ Custom Watchlist & Curated Market Tabs:**
  - Star (`★`) any stock, index, ETF, or cryptocurrency to track it in your personal **Watchlist**.
  - Instant tabs for **Watchlist**, **US Tech**, **India (NSE/BSE)**, **Crypto**, and **Global & Macro**.
- **🔎 Live Global Ticker Search:**
  - Search any company name or ticker symbol worldwide (`NVDA`, `AAPL`, `RELIANCE.NS`, `TCS.NS`, `BTC-USD`, `GC=F`) with live autocomplete.
- **🕯️ Interactive Touch-Scrubbing Financial Charts:**
  - Switch between **Area/Line Charts** and **OHLC Candlestick Charts**.
  - Multi-timeframe support: **`1D` (5m intraday)**, **`5D`**, **`1M`**, **`6M`**, **`1Y`**, and **`5Y`**.
  - **Touch-to-Scrub Crosshair:** Drag your finger across any chart to inspect the exact historical price and timestamp.
- **📊 Built-In Technical Indicators (RSI-14 & SMA-20):**
  - Displays a **20-Period Simple Moving Average (SMA-20)** overlay directly on the chart.
  - Computes **14-Period Relative Strength Index (RSI)**, automated momentum signals (`BULLISH`, `OVERBOUGHT`, `OVERSOLD`), and a **52-Week Range Progress Bar**.
- **📰 Real-Time Ticker News:**
  - Fetches live financial news articles specific to whichever stock or asset you are inspecting.
- **🔔 Daily Watchlist Updates & Custom Price Target Alerts (`WorkManager`):**
  - Sends periodic background notifications summarizing your Watchlist's daily price movements and alerts you when an asset crosses your custom target price.
